# Creative Playbooks and Shot Examples

This file translates the discussion into creative operating rules. Product examples and scripts below are planning illustrations, not manufacturer facts, proven hooks, or measured winners.

## 1. Category playbooks

| Category | Adult viewer tension to test | Product action | Purchase information to show | Initial recipe fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kitchen preparation | How does this tool fit the preparation task? | Load/setup → operate → inspect output | Capacity/contents/control/cleanup only where verified | Hands demo, creator demo, unbox/first use. |
| Cleaning tools | Can I inspect what the tool actually does in this situation? | Contact with the relevant surface/material → operation → visible state | Attachment/contact/handling and bounded observable finish | Hands demo or creator demo. |
| Large-piece building toy | What can be built and how does play continue afterward? | Open → choose parts → connect → completed build → play | Included components, age, build/use mechanism | Unbox/build/play. |
| Pretend-play toy | What would an actual play episode look like? | Open → arrange → one role-play action → reset/repeat | Contents, physical interaction, storage if verified | Unbox/first play. |
| Take-apart toy | What is connected, and does the assembled object work as shown? | Inspect parts → connect → tighten if appropriate → operate/play | Assembly reference, tool/accessories, completed configuration, age | Unbox/build/play with stronger action references. |

The exact SKU and evidence determine age suitability and feasible actions. Category names do not authorize blanket learning, attention-span, developmental, safety, or screen-time claims.

For ages 3–4, initially prefer a small number of clear actions and easy-to-follow play. For ages 5–6, a more involved build can be considered where the actual product permits it. These are creative planning hypotheses; manufacturer age guidance remains authoritative for product selection.

## 2. Recipe versus creative mechanism

Recipe defines the executable production structure and constraints.

Mechanism defines why this particular adult viewer might continue watching: action-led curiosity, a concrete question, one purchase hesitation, an occasion, or the completed build/payoff.

For one toy SKU, examples of distinct tests are:

1. Opening curiosity → contents → first play.
2. A useful assembly action → completed build → how it plays.
3. Adult explanation of one actual buying consideration → matching close-up/demo.

Use the recipe that can truthfully execute the selected mechanism. Do not multiply production cards merely because several text concepts were suggested.

## 3. Adaptation from the Yapping Bible

Useful abstract starting points include:

- P03: an observable payoff, then product/action explanation.
- P04: explain while doing, with an intermediate state and completion.
- P06: a practical friction, a supported product format, handling/use, and a bounded close.
- P07: one concrete question answered through a matching part/mechanism view.

These are cross-category adaptations for testing. The Bible's source performance is unknown. Its English wellness/beauty examples do not establish local HomeGadget/toy conversion.

Source choice records one primary narrative pattern and, where available, complementary action/delivery observations. Preserve source IDs, approximate/exact timing labels, and the original attribution. Add category-specific references rather than claiming the old collection covers all toy assembly.

## 4. Rules that improve realism

Each shot needs a physical task: peel a seal, lift a lid, spread pieces, align two parts, press a control, turn the finished object, or demonstrate one play action.

Specify where the hand starts and ends, what part it contacts, the product state before/after, and why the camera can see the useful detail. Write delivery instructions as gestures and pauses tied to the task.

Product photos should lock actual appearance. Operation footage/manuals should lock mechanics. An approved creator anchor should lock the adult identity. An exact local package image should lock visible packaging.

Naturalness comes from situation, useful action, comfortable speech, and coherent sound. Do not fill prompts with unsupported adjectives or script every second with enthusiastic claims.

Preserve believable pauses for handling and inspection. Let object sounds carry a reveal/connect action where appropriate. Cut when a useful new view, action, thought, or state enters.

Generated demonstrations do not verify measured chopping speed, cleaning efficacy, durability, or learning outcomes. Do not use AI-created before/after as independent performance evidence. Quantitative/efficacy claims require supplied supporting evidence and limitations.

## 5. Example A — HomeGadget demo, 30 seconds

Illustrative brief:

- Recipe: HOME_HANDS_DEMO.
- Locale: id-ID.
- One exact chopper model/listing, capacity/accessories not assumed.
- Required assets: product appearance, verified loading/operation reference, relevant output-state reference.
- Authorized facts in the example: F_OPERATION describes approved operation; F_CONTROL identifies the actual control; F_OUTPUT describes a supplied bounded observation. Replace them with actual facts before generation.
- No numeric speed claim, discount, or long-term testimonial.

| Time | Beat purpose | Shot/action and state | Illustrative spoken direction | Edit/sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Establish a reason to inspect the tool | Close view of the exact product and ingredients ready for the verified operation | “Lihat hasil potongannya dulu.” | A short useful preview is allowed only if it comes from the same product/conditions; mark it as a preview in the edit plan. |
| 3–7s | Make setup understandable | Load the specified ingredients using the verified procedure; READY → LOADED | “Ini cara masukin bahannya.” | Hold long enough to inspect the setup; include object contact sound. |
| 7–13s | Show operation | Correct lid/control handling; LOADED → OPERATING | Dialogue names the actual operation/control using F_OPERATION/F_CONTROL | Voice does not cover every moment; use verified relevant sound. |
| 13–20s | Inspect observable output | Open only when appropriate and show the supported output; OPERATING → OUTPUT_VISIBLE | Describe only the bounded observation authorized by F_OUTPUT | No unrelated before/after or invented stopwatch. |
| 20–26s | Answer one practical buying consideration | Show the exact feature/accessory or handling detail that answers the supplied tension | One authorized feature sentence, or a silent inspection if no fact supports it | Useful close-up rather than a redundant beauty shot. |
| 26–30s | Give one next step | Product at rest, supported outcome visible, destination metadata bound | “Cek varian dan detail produknya di link.” | CTA wording follows the selected placement; price is omitted unless current and authorized. |

This is a starting allocation, not an analysis of the user's sample. Actual dialogue and audio preview can change the intervals. If precise operating mechanics cannot be reliably generated, use authorized real action footage for the critical demonstration and review the mixed result for continuity.

## 6. Example B — Toy unbox/build/play, 30 seconds

Illustrative brief:

- Recipe: TOY_UNBOX_BUILD_PLAY.
- One exact large-piece vehicle-building set, age suitability and completed vehicle confirmed by the actual product evidence.
- Adult creator, with hands demonstrating the build. No fabricated child reaction or personal parenting history.
- Required states: BOX_CLOSED, CONTENTS_VISIBLE, BUILD_STAGE_1, BUILD_STAGE_2, BUILD_COMPLETE, PLAY.
- Required facts: F_CONTENTS, F_BUILD_MECHANISM, F_PLAY_MECHANISM; F_AGE if spoken/on-screen.
- Every visible part and connection comes from the actual SKU/action references.

| Time | Beat purpose | Shot/action and state | Illustrative spoken direction | Edit/sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Set up assembly curiosity | Adult begins opening the exact package; BOX_CLOSED → BOX_OPEN | “Gue rakit yang ini dulu. Lihat hasilnya.” | Natural packaging sound; no unsupported claim about difficulty. |
| 3–7s | Show what the buyer receives | Spread the actual included pieces; BOX_OPEN → CONTENTS_VISIBLE | Identify the build target only if supplied | Never invent or visually inflate part count. |
| 7–12s | Show one understandable connection | Align and connect the referenced base parts; CONTENTS_VISIBLE → BUILD_STAGE_1 | “Mulai dari bagian ini.” | Pause during connection; hands and contact must remain plausible. |
| 12–18s | Advance the build coherently | Show the next necessary assembly action; BUILD_STAGE_1 → BUILD_STAGE_2 | Explain the actual next step, or use object sound | An edit can omit repetitive steps while preserving correct intermediate/final states. |
| 18–23s | Deliver the completed build | Finish the demonstrated configuration; BUILD_STAGE_2 → BUILD_COMPLETE | “Sekarang bentuknya sudah kelihatan.” | Hold the result so the viewer can inspect it. |
| 23–27s | Show the play mechanism | Move/use the assembled toy exactly as its reference supports; BUILD_COMPLETE → PLAY | One short F_PLAY_MECHANISM line, if helpful | Keep the product scale and components stable. |
| 27–30s | Help the adult make the next decision | Rest the toy beside the package | “Cek isi paket dan rekomendasi usianya di detail produk.” | Bind the actual listing; avoid invented urgency. |

This is an edited demonstration of selected assembly actions. It must not claim that the physical toy was completely built in thirty seconds. If a larger build cannot be communicated clearly, choose a simpler supported configuration or a longer approved duration.

The creator can react to the current reveal/action. A generated creator cannot say “anak gue fokus dua jam” or invent a child's endorsement.

## 7. Example C — Toy unbox/first play, 30 seconds

Illustrative brief:

- Recipe: TOY_UNBOX_FIRST_PLAY.
- One compact pretend-play set with confirmed pieces and age evidence.
- Choose one simple role-play situation from the actual set. A toy kitchen scene does not imply real heat, food safety, or powered functions.
- Opening, arranging, one play episode, and practical close must be visually distinct.

| Time | Purpose | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Give the viewer a concrete curiosity | Reveal the item that makes the selected play episode possible. |
| 4–9s | Establish actual contents | Place the included pieces in a readable arrangement. |
| 9–20s | Show how play works | Adult demonstrates one coherent role-play action using those pieces. |
| 20–26s | Show a practical consideration | Inspect handling or storage only if the exact product supports it. |
| 26–30s | Direct the adult buyer | Show package/product and point to actual contents/age/variant information. |

Write the spoken script after the exact play mechanism and facts are available. Generic lines about imagination or intelligence are not a substitute for showing the product.

## 8. Five-market adaptation

Keep the product action and actual facts stable where the physical SKU matches. Localize speech, pronunciation, idiom, units, readable text, and CTA for the selected market/placement.

Local product packaging and offers can change the shots. A different included set changes the physical SKU and assembly plan. A local market has its own performance history; translated scripts are untested hypotheses until measured.

Do not infer character ethnicity/nationality from market. Use the operator's approved creator configuration.

Malay is not obtained by renaming an Indonesian voice. Thai requires real speech/glyph/segmentation review. Singapore English and US English can use distinct pronunciation/delivery where the configured voice and brief support it.

## 9. Controlled creative variations

Start from an accepted product/creator/audio/reference foundation:

- Hook mechanism test: keep the main demonstration, destination, locale, and distribution context stable.
- Recipe test: keep SKU/listing/market/objective stable and compare a clearly declared presentation change.
- Buyer-tension test: change the argument and matching demonstration where needed; treat the result as an angle test.
- Locale/market expansion: record a new context rather than claiming an isolated hook test.

A hook-only variation can reuse body footage when fact/offer/voice/edit compatibility remains valid. A changed build or offer may require new footage/audio and another final review.

The system should explain what is being tested and the evidence available. It should not call all variants winning before they run.
