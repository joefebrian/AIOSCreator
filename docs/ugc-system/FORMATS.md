# UGC + Slideshow Format Library

## 1. Proof-first demo
Best for apps, tools, visual transformations, and utilities.

1. Show the result or surprising action immediately.
2. One-line hook.
3. Recreate the input/problem.
4. Show product being used.
5. Show the output.
6. One proof/detail that makes the result believable.
7. CTA.

Test: first frame, result shown, hook wording, reveal timing.

## 2. “I found this” creator UGC
1. Natural creator shot, already mid-thought.
2. Discovery hook.
3. Explain the old frustration in one beat.
4. Show the product naturally.
5. Demonstrate the main feature.
6. Explain why it is useful.
7. CTA.

Keep this conversational. Avoid sounding like a scripted testimonial.

## 3. Problem → mechanism → payoff
1. Problem visual.
2. Specific pain hook.
3. Why the usual method is annoying.
4. Introduce product mechanism.
5. Show it working.
6. Payoff/result.
7. CTA.

## 4. Screen-record walkthrough
1. Result on screen first.
2. Hook text above UI.
3. Tap/scroll through 2–4 steps.
4. Zoom into the feature creating the payoff.
5. Show final output.
6. CTA overlay.

Rules: keep UI readable, crop dead space, do not cover important UI, and only add voiceover when it adds information.

## 5. Before / after
1. Before state.
2. Hook naming the problem.
3. Product/process.
4. After state.
5. Explain the one change that caused it.
6. CTA.

Do not fabricate before/after results.

## 6. Comparison test
1. Side-by-side setup.
2. State the same input/rules.
3. Show option A.
4. Show option B.
5. Reveal result.
6. Creator reaction/conclusion.
7. CTA.

## 7. Native slideshow: discovery
Slide 1: curiosity hook + strongest visual
Slide 2: old problem
Slide 3: “then I found…” transition
Slide 4: product / app visual
Slide 5: strongest feature
Slide 6: second proof/detail
Slide 7: payoff / who it is for
Slide 8: CTA

Keep each slide to one thought. Most slides should be 5–14 words, not paragraphs.

## 8. Native slideshow: list
Slide 1: “3/5 things…” hook
Slide 2: item 1
Slide 3: item 2
Slide 4: item 3
Slide 5: optional item 4
Slide 6: product naturally appears as one item/mechanism
Slide 7: summary / strongest recommendation
Slide 8: CTA

The product should earn its place in the list. Do not make every slide a disguised ad.

## 9. Native slideshow: story
Slide 1: tension / outcome hook
Slide 2: context
Slide 3: first failure/problem
Slide 4: turning point
Slide 5: product discovery
Slide 6: what changed
Slide 7: result
Slide 8: CTA / final thought

## 10. Comment-reply UGC
1. Real question/comment on screen.
2. Creator answers immediately.
3. Show the exact step/product being asked about.
4. Short demonstration.
5. One extra useful detail.
6. CTA only if natural.

Use real recurring questions or truthful FAQs. Do not fabricate social proof.
