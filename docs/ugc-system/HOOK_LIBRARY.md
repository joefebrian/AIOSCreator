# Hook Library

Use these as structures, not copy-paste lines. Rewrite them so they sound native to the creator, product, and audience.

## Discovery hooks
- “I found the [thing] that finally ___.”
- “Why did nobody tell me you could ___?”
- “I genuinely didn’t know there was an app that ___.”
- “This is the first time I’ve seen ___ done like this.”
- “I wish I found this before I ___.”

## Pain-first hooks
- “If you keep struggling with ___, try this.”
- “The most annoying part about ___ is ___.”
- “I was doing ___ completely wrong.”
- “I stopped doing ___ manually after finding this.”
- “This fixed the part of ___ I hated most.”

## Outcome hooks
- “This is how I went from ___ to ___.”
- “I finally got ___ without ___.”
- “The easiest way I’ve found to ___.”
- “This made ___ take me 5 minutes instead of an hour.”
- “Here’s what changed when I started ___.”

## Proof hooks
- “Watch what happens when I ___.”
- “I didn’t believe this worked, so I tested it.”
- “Here’s the before. Here’s what it did.”
- “I gave it ___ and it returned ___.”
- “No explanation, just watch this.”

## Curiosity / open-loop hooks
- “The last part is why I kept using it.”
- “I thought this was a gimmick until ___.”
- “There’s one feature here I haven’t seen anywhere else.”
- “This looked normal until I pressed ___.”
- “I wasn’t expecting the result to look like this.”

## Contrarian hooks
- “You probably don’t need another ___.”
- “Stop doing ___ like this.”
- “The popular way to ___ is slower.”
- “I’d skip ___ and do this instead.”
- “Most people focus on ___ when ___ matters more.”

## Comparison hooks
- “I tested ___ vs ___ so you don’t have to.”
- “What happens if you use ___ instead of ___?”
- “Same input. Two completely different results.”
- “Before ___ / after ___.”
- “Manual vs using ___.”

## Identity hooks
- “If you’re a ___, save this.”
- “Every ___ eventually runs into this.”
- “For anyone trying to ___ without ___.”
- “If your ___ looks like this, do this.”
- “This is for people who ___.”

## List hooks
- “3 things I wish I knew before ___.”
- “3 ways to ___ faster.”
- “The 4 mistakes killing your ___.”
- “5 tools I’d use if I had to restart ___.”
- “My current ___ stack.”

## Comment / reply hooks
- “People keep asking how I ___.”
- “Replying to everyone asking about ___.”
- “Okay, here’s exactly how I did it.”
- “For the people asking what I used…”
- “This is the setup from my last post.”

## Hook variation matrix
For any strong angle, generate at least:
- 3 direct hooks
- 3 curiosity hooks
- 2 proof hooks
- 2 pain hooks
- 2 identity hooks

Then choose the strongest 4 based on how quickly the product can deliver the promised payoff.

## Hook quality check
A hook passes when:
1. It is understandable without context.
2. It creates a reason to watch the next frame.
3. The creative can actually pay it off.
4. It sounds like something the creator could naturally say.
5. It does not rely on a false or unprovable claim.
